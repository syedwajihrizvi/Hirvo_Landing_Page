Zoom Help

Effective Date: April 4, 2026

Hirvo uses OAuth 2.0 to integrate with Zoom for secure authentication and authorization. This guide provides an overview of how to set up and use the Zoom integration within the Hirvo app. By connecting your Zoom account, you can seamlessly create and manage meetings directly from Hirvo, enhancing your scheduling and communication experience. The Zoom integration allows you to easily schedule interviews, team meetings, and other events without leaving the Hirvo platform, streamlining your workflow and improving efficiency.

a. How to Add Zoom Integration

  • When scheduling an online interviw, choose your desire platform as Zoom
  • On the OAuth Consent screen, give Hirvo permission to create, update, and delete meetings on your behalf
  • Whenver you create an interview on Hirvo, if Hirvo has consent it will automatically create the corresponding Zoom meeting
  • Troubleshooting: If you encounter any issues with the Zoom integration, please contact our support team for assistance at support@hirvo.ca.

b. How to Remove Zoom Integration

  • There are 2 ways to remove the Zoom integration
  • On the Schedule Interview screen, you can Sign Out of Zoom. This will remove all tokens that grant Hirvo access to your Zoom account.
  • If you sign out of the app, it will also remove all tokens that grant Hirvo access to your Zoom account.
